## Findings remaining in source

### 1. Bracketed nickname interrupts room rendering

[coop-main.ts](../../src/coop-main.ts), lines 164, 278 and 321, passes
dynamic names into Kaplay text. [ws.ts](../../server/coop/ws.ts), lines
62–64, accepts bracketed names. A created a room as [Sun] and B joined by
invitation in independent local profiles. Both received "Styled text error:
unclosed tags Sun" from makeLabel → ensurePlayer → applyState.
The player's selfPos stayed null and the player list stayed empty after
movement input; the server remained running. HTML escaping at the DOM sink
does not protect Kaplay's separate text grammar. Local process/profile/data
cleanup was completed.

### 2. Successful-card logs omit the card and echo unvalidated target text

[ws.ts](../../server/coop/ws.ts), lines 545 and 571–575, carries client
targetKey into the log. Only single-target cards validate that field in
[fight-session.ts](../../server/coop/fight-session.ts), lines 397–400.
A legal Guard with an irrelevant 2048-character targetKey succeeded and
the capturing sink received the entire string, without a card-name field.
This limits both log bounds and the ability to narrate the actual play.

### 3. Forced-flee log reports a failure as success

[ws.ts](../../server/coop/ws.ts), lines 514–517, ignores forceFlee's
result. The timer callback for an offline downed player emitted
coop.fight.flee with result=ok although the player remained downed and
fled=false. The rule correctly rejects that operation at
[fight-session.ts](../../server/coop/fight-session.ts), lines 436–440.
The caller's log must distinguish request acceptance, completion and rejection.

### 4. Rejected request changes the advertised last-accepted sequence

[fight-session.ts](../../server/coop/fight-session.ts), line 390, advances
lastSeq before checking card ownership, cost and target. An invalid card at
seq=2 was rejected but changed lastSeq to 2; a legal End Turn with that same
sequence was then rejected as stale. The
[protocol description](../../src/coop-protocol.ts), lines 148–152, calls this
the last-accepted sequence. This is a contract mismatch, not evidence that
another player's resources changed. Choose and document either processed-
request or accepted-action semantics and verify client recovery.

### 5. Wiped player lacks a visible continuation

[fight-session.ts](../../server/coop/fight-session.ts), line 520,
clears occupancy while retaining zero HP;
[room.ts](../../server/coop/room.ts), lines 174 and 218, permits movement
but excludes the dead player from encounters.
[coop-main.ts](../../src/coop-main.ts), line 871, only reports "You fell."
Actual isolated room/fight logic produced wiped, hp=0, fightId=null,
33 units moved, unchanged zero HP on rejoin and no new fight. No full
browser end-of-run check was performed for this finding. A clear expedition
end and new-room path is the proposed small follow-up, not a new revival system.
